A true mirror on your computer: see your face the way other people see it, not flipped like a bathroom mirror. Check your lighting, line your face up and save a straight, well-lit photo, all on your own device.

TRUE MIRROR AND WEBCAM MIRROR
• Switch between the mirror view (flipped, the face you are used to) and the true view: the non-reversed mirror that shows how other people and cameras see you.
• Zoom up to 3×, a white light ring that brightens your face on screen, and a 3 or 10 second timer.

FACE GUIDE
• An oval with a centre line, an eye line and a thirds grid, so your face is level, centred and straight-on.

LIGHTING CHECK
• Reads the light inside the face guide: is it bright enough, is one side darker, is part of the face blown out?
• Tells you what to change: "turn toward the light", "move closer to the light", "soften the light".

FREEZE AND SAVE
• Freeze the frame and save it as a JPEG in your downloads, as the camera sees you or mirrored.

FLIP IMAGE
• Drop, choose or paste any picture of yourself and see it as saved and flipped, side by side. Save the flipped version.

MEASURE IT (OPTIONAL)
• One click opens a free measurement on thefaceanalysis.com: face symmetry, face shape, golden ratio, facial thirds, eye shape, canthal tilt, jawline. You upload the photo there yourself; the extension itself measures nothing and uploads nothing.

PRIVACY
The camera picture and your photos never leave your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no access to the websites you visit. Open source (MIT): github.com/The-Face-Analysis/thefaceanalysis-extension
